FAQ

What kinds of projects are a good fit for Wonder Makers?
Our core areas include high-end websites and digital experiences, applications and SaaS platforms, premium e-commerce, and Web3 or AI products.
What does a project like this typically cost?
It depends on scope and complexity. Focused projects start from $10,000, full web presences range $40,000–$150,000, and end-to-end digital products start at $80,000.
How do you approach pricing and project models?
We usually offer fixed-price for well-defined scopes and flexible models for evolving work. Discovery phases are recommended when scope is still unclear.
We're working with a tight deadline – how fast can you move?
Landing pages can launch in as little as 14 days, full web presences in 6–10 weeks, and digital products in 3–6 months.
Will we actually work with the senior team we meet at the start?
Yes. Wonder Makers is senior-led, so the team you meet early remains responsible for delivery.
How is your team structured, and how do you handle international collaboration?
We are Prague-based, working remotely with clients globally using tools like Slack, Figma, and project management platforms.
Can you work alongside our internal team?
Yes — our senior specialists integrate directly into your team with clear ownership across responsibilities.
We don't have a clear brief yet – can we still start?
Yes. We typically begin with a Discovery & Definition phase to shape the brief and validate feasibility.
Do you help shape the product, or mainly execute on briefs?
Strategy is the first service we offer. We help refine the problem before executing the solution.
Is your team experienced enough to handle complex, large-scale technical projects?
Yes — we build scalable backend architectures, real-time platforms, SaaS products, multi-chain Web3 applications, and performance-critical systems.
How do you approach AI in your work?
We use AI selectively for ideation and prototyping, with senior review and transparency on any AI-assisted output.
What happens after launch?
Many clients continue with ongoing development, optimisation, analytics, and iterative UX improvements.
